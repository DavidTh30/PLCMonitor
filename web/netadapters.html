Type the PLC's address: PLC Monitor checks whether this PC is in its network, finds a free address for this PC next to it and adds it to the adapter on the PLC's cable.
One or more addresses, separated by commas or spaces; a range like 192.168.0.10-20 (up to 64). A device that doesn't answer may still be there with ping blocked.
Settings saved with Save in an adapter's forms – e.g. one per machine or customer. Apply one with a click.
